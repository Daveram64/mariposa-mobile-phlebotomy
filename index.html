What areas do you serve?

Sonoma, Marin, Napa, Solano, and Sacramento counties, plus the greater San Francisco Bay Area, including Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Mateo.

Do you take my insurance?

We do not bill insurance directly, but we provide a detailed superbill you can submit to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement.

Are you certified and HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Our lead phlebotomist is California CPT-1 certified with an ER background, and Mariposa is fully insured and HIPAA compliant.

Do you do pediatric or autism-informed draws?

Yes. We offer gentle pediatric draws and autism-informed, sensory-aware care in the comfort of home, using calming techniques to reduce needle anxiety.

How do I prepare for my draw?

Follow any fasting instructions from your provider, drink water beforehand, and have your lab order ready. We handle the rest at your door.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online at mariposamobilephlebotomy.as.me or call or text (707) 280-3651. Same-day and next-day appointments are often available.
