Calm draws

Scared of needles? You're exactly who we built this for.

You never have to sit in a waiting room getting more nervous by the minute. We come to you, we listen first, and nothing happens until you're ready.

  • Your space, not a lab

    Your couch, your bed, your favorite chair. No fluorescent lights, no line, no strangers watching.

  • Your pace

    Talk us through what scares you. Want every step explained, or nothing at all? You choose.

  • Comfort tools

    We bring a Buzzy vibration device to quiet the feeling of the poke, and we can draw with you lying down.

  • One smooth stick

    ER-trained hands that are used to tough veins, so it's quick and done right the first time.

  • Fainters welcome

    If you've passed out before, tell us. We plan for it and stay with you after the draw.

  • Done in minutes

    Most visits take 15 to 20 minutes. We handle the tubes, the paperwork, and the drop-off at the lab.

Autism-friendly draws

A blood draw that works with your child, not against them.

A clinic can be loud, bright, and unpredictable. At home, your child is surrounded by what's familiar, and we build the visit around how they experience the world.

  1. 1

    We plan together first.

    Before the visit, tell us about sensory needs, triggers, the words that help, and what calms them.

  2. 2

    Two pediatric specialists.

    Children's draws are done by two phlebotomists trained for little ones, on quiet Saturday mornings.

  3. 3

    You stay in charge.

    We coach you on how to support your child, and we bring a Buzzy and numbing spray to make it gentler.

  4. 4

    We follow your flow.

    Every child and family is different. If you want help with a comfort hold or positioning, we follow your direction and your child's cues, gently. We never force a draw: if your child can't go ahead, we stop and reschedule.

Autistic adults are welcome too. Tell us what you need, whether that's a quiet visit, no small talk, a set routine, or a heads-up before every step.

Questions

Calm draws, answered.

I faint during blood draws. Can you still help?

Yes. Tell us when you book. We plan for it, we can draw with you lying down, and we stay with you after the draw.

How long does a calm draw take?

Most visits take 15 to 20 minutes, and nothing starts until you're ready. We handle the tubes, the paperwork, and the drop-off at your lab.

Does a calm draw cost more?

No. Calm draws use the same price list as every visit: $200 for a home draw, $300 for a pediatric draw, and +$25 for autism-informed care. See the full price list.

Do you see autistic adults?

Yes. Tell us what you need, whether that's a quiet visit, no small talk, a set routine, or a heads-up before every step.

What if my child can't go ahead?

We stop. No holding down, ever. We reschedule, and you pay only the visit fee. The visit fee is $50; the rest is refunded.
