How it works

How it works.

Other kits

Other mail-in kits.

We handle other at-home lab kits the same way: InsideTracker, Function Health, Everlywell, Genova, Boston Heart, Vibrant, and similar. Each kit's instructions differ (some need the sample spun and shipped cold), so tell us which kit you have when you book and we'll come prepared.

Non-Galleri kit draws are our standard $200 rate, kit handling included.

Questions

Galleri, answered.

Do I need to fast for Galleri?

No. Galleri does not require fasting. Drink water and eat normally.

Do you need the kit before you book?

Book once the kit is in your hands. If it hasn't arrived by your appointment, text us and we'll move the slot at no charge.

Can you spin and cold-pack the sample?

Yes. We bring what the kit needs. Galleri itself ships at room temperature; kits that need a centrifuge spin and cold pack get exactly that.

When do results come back?

Galleri results go to your ordering provider, usually within about two weeks of the lab receiving the sample. Other kits vary; check the kit's own timeline.
