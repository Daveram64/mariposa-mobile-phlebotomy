Getting paid back

Insurance, Medicare, and superbills.

We don't bill insurance directly. You pay when you book, on our secure scheduling page, and we give you everything you need to seek reimbursement on your own.

A superbill is an itemized receipt with the procedure and diagnosis codes your insurer needs to consider an out-of-network claim. Submit it with your insurer's out-of-network claim form, and keep a copy for your records. Many PPO plans reimburse part of the visit, so it's worth checking your plan's out-of-network benefits before you book.

Medicare does not reimburse our visit fee. The lab still bills Medicare for the tests themselves as usual.

Plans change

Cancellation and reschedule policy.

Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours, or if we arrive and the draw can't go ahead, a $50 visit fee applies and the rest is refunded. If we have to cancel, you are never charged. Full terms and cancellation policy.

Questions

Pricing questions.

How much does a mobile blood draw cost?

A standard home draw is $200. Pediatric draws are $200 to $250, and autism-informed care adds $25. Therapeutic phlebotomy is $250 per unit draw, a Galleri test draw is $175 with kit handling, and athlete performance packages are $450, $550, or $700. Every price is listed before you book, and each one covers the visit, the draw, labeling, and delivery to your lab. See the full price list.

Is there a travel fee?

No travel fee anywhere in our service area: Sonoma, Marin, Napa, Solano, Sacramento, and the greater Bay Area. Outside that area, call us and we'll quote the visit before you book.

How do I pay?

You pay on our scheduling page when you book, by card. You receive a receipt and, after the visit, a superbill.

Do you take insurance or Medicare?

We do not bill insurance or Medicare directly. You pay when you book, and we give you a superbill: an itemized receipt with the procedure codes your insurer needs to consider out-of-network reimbursement. Many PPO plans reimburse part of the visit.

Medicare does not reimburse our visit fee. The lab still bills Medicare for the tests themselves as usual.
