Therapeutic phlebotomy

Therapeutic phlebotomy, at home.

If your doctor has ordered regular blood removal for hemochromatosis, polycythemia vera, or another iron or red-cell condition, we do the draw where you live. One unit per visit, on your provider's order, and we keep the standing order on file so the series just gets scheduled.

Who this is for

Provider-ordered unit draws.

  • Hereditary hemochromatosis (iron overload)
  • Polycythemia vera and secondary polycythemia
  • Porphyria cutanea tarda
  • Any other order where your provider prescribes removal of a unit of blood

We follow your provider's order for volume and frequency. We do not decide either one ourselves, and we never draw without a current order.

The visit

How a visit goes.

  1. 1

    Send us the order

    Text or email a photo of the order, or have your provider's office fax it. Standing orders are welcome: tell us the frequency and we'll book the whole series.

  2. 2

    We arrive with everything

    Collection bag, scale, and supplies. You sit or recline in your own chair. We check your blood pressure and pulse first, and the draw itself takes 10 to 20 minutes.

  3. 3

    Aftercare and disposal

    We stay until you're steady, go over fluids and rest for the day, and take the unit with us for disposal as medical waste. Nothing is left in your home.

Get ready

Before your draw.

  • Drink water through the morning and eat a normal meal, unless your provider says otherwise
  • Have your current medication list handy
  • Wear a top with sleeves that push up easily
  • Feeling unwell that day? Text us and we reschedule at no charge

Pricing

What it costs.

$250 per unit draw, including the visit, supplies, and disposal. A standing-order series is the same rate per visit, and a superbill is provided after each one. We do not bill insurance or Medicare directly.

Questions

Therapeutic phlebotomy, answered.

Do I need a new order every time?

No. A standing order from your provider covers the series. We keep it on file and book each visit against it until it expires or your provider changes it.

How much blood is removed?

Your provider's order sets the volume. A typical therapeutic draw removes one unit, about 450 to 500 mL, and we weigh the bag to hit the ordered amount.

Is it safe to do at home?

Yes, when your provider has ordered it and you're well that day. We check your blood pressure and pulse before we start, stay for the rest period after, and stop the draw if you feel lightheaded.

Can you draw my labs at the same visit?

Yes. If your provider also orders labs (ferritin, hematocrit, and so on), we draw them at the same visit and deliver them to your lab.
