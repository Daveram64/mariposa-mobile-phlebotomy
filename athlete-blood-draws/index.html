Questions

Athlete draws, answered.

Which tier should I pick?

Baseline if it's your first panel or you want a cheap checkpoint. Performance if you're in season and watching hormones, thyroid, and inflammation. Complete before a big block or when something feels off and you want the whole picture. Call or text and we'll help you choose.

Do I have to fast?

Yes, 10 to 12 hours, water only. Skip the morning workout until after the draw.

Do I need a doctor's order?

No. The packages include the lab order.

Can I get a superbill?

Yes, for every package. Performance panels are often not covered by insurance, but the superbill is yours to submit.
